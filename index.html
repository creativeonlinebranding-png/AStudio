Vision Statement

1Africa Radio TV is a multi-lingual mass communication platform where radio and television meet and work together to create Pan-African content. It is aimed at rebuilding the African brand from the failures of the past to the emergent new Africa of today—one that is fearless, mentally liberated, focused, goal-driven, and free to be who they want to be. One of our main aims is to tell the new Africa story to the world and bring Africa together. We are the gateway to the world; hence, we call ourselves “Africa Embassy.”

Our Motto: “Africa, we are one.”

Mission Statement

1Africa Radio TV’s mission is to empower the African child and the underprivileged young generation with skill sets, empowerment, and upliftment through quality teaching. Our main mission is to bridge the gap between education and employment by equipping young individuals with practical, in-demand skills needed for success in today’s dynamic job market.

1Africa Radio TV Company History

Founded in 2018, 1Africa Radio TV emerged as a pioneering Pan-African multimedia platform dedicated to celebrating African culture, music, and storytelling. Starting as a single radio station in Johannesburg, we have grown into a continental network reaching 22 African nations. Our journey includes launching the first cross-border music chart show in 2020, establishing digital studios in four countries, and becoming the official media partner for the African Continental Free Trade Area in 2023.
